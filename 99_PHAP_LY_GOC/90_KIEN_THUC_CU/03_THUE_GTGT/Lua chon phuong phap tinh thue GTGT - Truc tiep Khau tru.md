# Lua chon phuong phap tinh thue GTGT: Truc tiep hay Khau tru?

> Nguon: Video TRANSCRIPT (LuatVietnam)

---

## 1. Hai phuong phap co ban

### 1.1. Phuong phap khau tru
- **Nguyen tac**: Thue GTGT dau vao duoc khau tru khoi thue GTGT dau ra
- **Ap dung**: Doanh nghiep lon, co doanh thu lon
- **Uu diem**: Khong phai dong thue cao, chi dong chenh lech

### 1.2. Phuong phap truc tiep
- **Nguyen tac**: Tinh thue GTGT truc tiep tren doanh thu
- **Ap dung**: Doanh nghiep nho, co doanh thu nho
- **Uu diem**: Don gian, de quan ly

---

## 2. So sanh

| Tieu chi | Khau tru | Truc tiep |
|---|---|---|
| Doanh thu | Lon | Nho |
| Do phuc tap | Cao | Thap |
| Tinh chinh xac | Cao | Thap |
| De quan ly | Kho | De |

---

## 3. Cach lua chon

### 3.1. Tieu chi lua chon
- **Doanh thu**: Lon hon nguong thi dung khau tru
- **Quy mo**: Doanh nghiep lon nen dung khau tru
- **Nang luc ke toan**: Can nang luc ke toan tot

### 3.2. Nguyen tac
- **Doanh thu > 1 ty dong/nam**: Nen dung khau tru
- **Doanh thu < 1 ty dong/nam**: Co the dung truc tiep

---

## 4. Luu y thuc te

1. **Cap nhat**: Thay doi phuong phap khi doanh thu thay doi
2. **Tu van**: Hoi y kien chuyen vien truoc khi quyet dinh
3. **Luu tru**: Giu lai chung tu chung minh lua chon
