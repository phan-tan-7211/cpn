# Chi tieu 34A tren to khai GTGT Mau 01 - Thong tu 89/2024/TT-BTC

> Nguon: Video TRANSCRIPT (Ke toan thue)

---

## 1. Tong quan

- **Chi tieu 34A**: Ghi nhan so tien thue GTGT duoc khau tru trong ky
- **Ap dung**: Tu 01/01/2025 theo Thong tu 89/2024/TT-BTC

---

## 2. Noi dung chi tiet

### 2.1. Cach tinh
- **Chi tieu 34A** = Thue GTGT dau vao duoc khau tru - Thue GTGT dau vao khong duoc khau tru

### 2.2. Cac truong hop cu the
- **Hoa don mua hang**: Ghi so tien thue GTGT tren hoa don
- **Dich vu**: Ghi so tien thue GTGT dich vu
- **Hang hoa nhap khau**: Ghi so tien thue GTGT nhap khau

### 2.3. Dieu kien khau tru
- Co hoa don GTGT hop le
- Hang hoa, dich vu su dung cho san xuat kinh doanh
- Chua vuot qua muc khau tru cho phep

---

## 3. Luu y quan trong

1. **Dung muc**: Khong ghi sai chi tieu
2. **Chung tu**: Dam bao co day du chung tu
3. **Thoi han**: Ke khai dung thoi han quy dinh

---

## 4. Vi du thuc te

- Mua nguyen lieu: 100 trieu + thue GTGT 10 trieu
- Chi tieu 34A: 10 trieu (thue GTGT duoc khau tru)
