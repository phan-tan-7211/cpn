# Nhan viec o doanh nghiep moi - Ban giao thong tin

---

## 1. Thong tin can nhan ban giao

### 1.1. Ho so phap ly
- **Giay phep kinh doanh**: Ban goc hoac ban sao cong
- **Dieu le**: Ban goc
- **Quyet dinh thanh lap**: Neu co

### 1.2. Ho so thue
- **Ma so thue**: Giay xac nhan MST
- **Ho so khai thue**: To khai cac ky truoc
- **Chung tu thue**: Hoa don, chung tu

### 1.3. Ho so ke toan
- **So ke toan**: So cai, so chi tiet
- **Chung tu ke toan**: Chung tu ngay, thang
- **Bao cao**: Bao cao tai chinh, bao cao thue

### 1.4. Ho so lao dong
- **Hop dong lao dong**: Cac hop dong HDTV
- **Quyen loi**: Bao hiem, phuc loi
- **Nghi phep**: Quy che nghi phep

---

## 2. Quy trinh ban giao

### 2.1. Lich ban giao
- **Ngay bat dau**: 01 ngay lam viec dau tien
- **Thoi han**: 05 ngay lam viec

### 2.2. Nguoi ban giao
- **Ke toan truong**: Hoac nguoi duoc uy quyen
- **Quan ly**: Xac nhan ban giao

### 2.3. Bang ban giao
- **Lap bang**: Ghi ro tung muc ban giao
- **Ky ten**: Ca hai ben
- **Luu tru**: Giu lai phieu ban giao

---

## 3. Luu y quan trong

1. **Day du**: Dam bao nhan du day du ho so
2. **Chinh xac**: Kiem tra tinh chinh xac cua thong tin
3. **Luu tru**: Ban giao va luu tru dung quy dinh
