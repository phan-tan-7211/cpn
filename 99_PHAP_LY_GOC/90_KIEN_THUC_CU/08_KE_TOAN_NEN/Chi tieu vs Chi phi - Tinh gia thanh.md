# Chi tieu vs Chi phi - Cach tinh gia thanh chinh xac

> Nguon: Video TRANSCRIPT (HocvienKeto)

---

## 1. Khai niem co ban

### 1.1. Chi phi (Cost)
- **Dinh nghia**: So tien thuc te da chi ra de san xuat, kinh doanh
- **Tinh chat**: Luon la so duong, khong am
- **Vi du**: Mua nguyen lieu 100 trieu -> Chi phi = 100 trieu

### 1.2. Chi tieu (Expense/Budget)
- **Dinh nghia**: So tien du kien, ke hoach chi tieu
- **Tinh chat**: Co the am (neu du kien thu nhap lon hon chi phi)
- **Vi du**: Du kien chi phi 80 trieu -> Chi tieu = 80 trieu

---

## 2. Su khac biet

| Tieu chi | Chi phi | Chi tieu |
|---|---|---|
| Tinh chat | Thuc te | Du kien |
| Thoi diem | Da xay ra | Chua xay ra |
| Tinh chat so tien | Luon duong | Co the am |
| Muc dich | Ghi nhan giao dich | Lap ke hoach |

---

## 3. Cach tinh gia thanh

### 3.1. Cong thuc co ban
- **Gia thanh** = Chi phi san xuat + Chi phi quan ly + Chi phi ban hang

### 3.2. Cach tinh theo tung loai
- **Gia thanh san pham** = Nguyen lieu + Nhan cong + Chi phi san xuat chung
- **Gia thanh dich vu** = Chi phi nhan cong + Chi phi ho tro

### 3.3. Luu y quan trong
- **Chi phi truc tiep**: Truc tiep lien quan den san pham
- **Chi phi gian tiep**: Chi phi quan ly, ho tro

---

## 4. Ap dung thuc te

1. **Phan biet ro rang**: Khong nham lan chi phi va chi tieu
2. **Ghi nhan dung thoi diem**: Chi phi khi da xay ra, chi tieu khi ke hoach
3. **Tinh gia thanh chinh xac**: Dam bao du day cac yeu to
