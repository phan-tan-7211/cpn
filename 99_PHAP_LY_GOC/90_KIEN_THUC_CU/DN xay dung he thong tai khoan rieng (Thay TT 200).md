# Doanh nghiep xay dung he thong tai khoan rieng (Thay the TT 200/2014/TT-BTC)

> Nguon: Video TRANSCRIPT (VAT-VN)

---

## 1. Tong quan

- **Thong tu 200/2014/TT-BTC**: Huong dan ke toan doanh nghiep
- **Thay the**: Tu 01/01/2025, DN co the xay dung he thong tai khoan rieng

---

## 2. Dieu kien

### 2.1. Phu hop
- He thong tai khoan phu hop voi hoat dong san xuat kinh doanh
- Dam bao tinh chinh xac, day du

### 2.2. Dang ky
- **Noi nop**: Cuc Thue
- **Thoi han**: 30 ngay ke tu ngay thanh lap
- **Ho so**: Don de nghi, He thong tai khoan

---

## 3. Quy trinh thuc hien

### Bước 1: Thiet lap he thong tai khoan
- Phan loai tai khoan theo nganh nghe
- Xac dinh ma so tai khoan

### Bước 2: Dang ky voi co quan thue
- Nop ho so dang ky
- Cho phep cua co quan thue

### Bước 3: Ap dung
- Ap dung tu ngay duoc phep
- Luu tru chung tu theo he thong moi

---

## 4. Luu y quan trong

1. **Phu hop**: Dam bao he thong phu hop voi hoat dong DN
2. **Dang ky**: Dang ky dung thoi han
3. **Luu tru**: Luu tru chung tu dung quy dinh
