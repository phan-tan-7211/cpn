# Ke khai thue voi HĐ thay the/đieu chinh | Ban mua

> Nguon: Video TRANSCRIPT (KNT)

---

## 1. Hoa don thay the

### Khi nao su dung
- Hoa don GTGT sai nhung **chua ke khai thue**
- Ban mua nhan duoc HĐ thay the tu ban ban

### Cach xu ly
- **Khong ke khai** HĐ cu (so 001GNRL)
- **Ke khai** HĐ thay the (so 002GNRL)
- Ghi vao muc tuong ung tren to khai GTGT

### Ke khai tren to khai
- **Muc 2**: Ghi so tien GTGT duoc khau tru tu HĐ thay the

---

## 2. Hoa don dieu chinh

### Khi nao su dung
- Hoa don GTGT sai nhung **da ke khai thue**
- Ban mua nhan duoc HĐ dieu chinh tu ban ban

### Cach xu ly
- **Ke khai** HĐ dieu chinh trong ky ke khai phat sinh
- **Giam** so tien GTGT duoc khau tru (neu giam)
- **Tang** so tien GTGT duoc khau tru (neu tang)

### Ke khai tren to khai
- **Muc 2**: Dieu chinh so tien GTGT khau tru

---

## 3. Luu y quan trong

1. **Hoa don thay the**: Chi ke khai HĐ thay the, khong ke khai HĐ cu
2. **Hoa don dieu chinh**: Ke khai trong ky phat sinh, dieu chinh so tien GTGT khau tru
3. **Thoi han**: Trong vong 05 ngay lam viec ke tu khi nhan duoc HĐ thay the/dieu chinh
