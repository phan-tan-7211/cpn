# Ke khai sai sot GTGT dau vao - Luat 48/2025

> Nguon: Video TRANSCRIPT (VAT-VN)

---

## 1. Khai niem

- **Sai sot GTGT dau vao**: Loi ke khai thue GTGT duoc khau tru tren to khai thue
- **Theo Luat 48/2025**: Co quy dinh moi ve xu ly sai sot

---

## 2. Cac loai sai sot

### 2.1. Sai sot khong anh huong
- Sai ten hang hoa, dia chi
- Sai so luong nho
- **Xu ly**: Dieu chinh trong ky ke khai phat sinh

### 2.2. Sai sot co anh huong
- Sai so tien thue GTGT
- Sai dieu kien khau tru
- **Xu ly**: Ke khai bo sung, nop phu

---

## 3. Quy trinh xu ly

### Bước 1: Phat hien sai sot
- Tu kiem tra hoac kiem toan
- Tu thong bao cua co quan thue

### Bước 2: Ke khai bo sung
- Lap to khai bo sung theo mau quy dinh
- Ghi ro loai sai sot va so tien

### Bước 3: Nop phu
- Nop tien phu vao ngan sach nha nuoc
- Co quan thue kiem tra va xac nhan

---

## 4. Luu y quan trong

1. **Thoi han**: Ke khai bo sung trong vong 60 ngay ke tu ngay phat hien
2. **Phat**: Co the bi phat neu cham ke khai
3. **Luu tru**: Giu lai chung tu chung minh
