## 2. Nghi dinh 181/2025 (01/11/2025)

### 2.1. Bo sung huong dan chi tiet
- **Khoan 3 Dieu 1**: Sua doi Khoan 2 Dieu 9 - Bo sung chung tu dien tu
- **Diem c Khoan 4 Dieu 1**: Bo sung "truong hop hang hoa, dich vu cung cap tren ung dung giao nhan"
- **Diem a Khoan 8 Dieu 1**: Bo sung ky hieu ky so cua tung hoa don

### 2.2. Cac truong hop cu the
- **Dich vu vien thong**: Thoi diem lap hoa don theo chu ky thanh toan
