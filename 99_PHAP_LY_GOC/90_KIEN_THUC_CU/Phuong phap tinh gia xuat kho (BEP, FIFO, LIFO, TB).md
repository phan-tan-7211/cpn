# Phuong phap tinh gia xuat kho (BEP, FIFO, LIFO, Trung binh)

> Nguon: Video TRANSCRIPT (Nha Thuat)

---

## 1. Tong quan

- **Gia xuat kho**: Gia tri kho hang khi xuat kho de san xuat hoac ban hang
- **Anh huong**: Anh huong den loi nhuan va thue TNDN

---

## 2. Cac phuong phap

### 2.1. FIFO (First In First Out)
- **Nguyen tac**: Hang nhap truoc xuat truoc
- **Uu diem**: Phan anh gia thuc te
- **Nhuoc diem**: Phuc tap trong quan ly

### 2.2. LIFO (Last In First Out)
- **Nguyen tac**: Hang nhap sau xuat truoc
- **Uu diem**: Don gian trong tinh toan
- **Nhuoc diem**: Khong phan anh gia thuc te

### 2.3. Trung binh binh quan
- **Nguyen tac**: Tinh gia trung binh cua tat ca hang nhap
- **Uu diem**: Don gian, de ap dung
- **Nhuoc diem**: Khong phan anh bien dong gia

### 2.4. BEP (Break Even Point)
- **Nguyen tac**: Tinh gia theo diem hoa von
- **Ap dung**: Trong ke hoach tai chinh

---

## 3. So sanh

| Phuong phap | Tinh chat | Ap dung |
|---|---|---|
| FIFO | Hang truoc xuat truoc | DN lon, kho lon |
| LIFO | Hang sau xuat truoc | DN nho, kho nho |
| Trung binh | Gia trung binh | DN trung binh |
| BEP | Diem hoa von | Ke hoach TC |

---

## 4. Luu y quan trong

1. **Chon phuong phap**: Phu hop voi hoat dong DN
2. **Ap dung nhat quan**: Khong thay doi thuong xuyen
3. **Luu tru**: Giu lai chung tu chung minh
